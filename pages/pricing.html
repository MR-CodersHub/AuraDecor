1

Consultation

Honest Craft Economics

What Determines Our
Handmade Prices?

We believe in transparent pricing that values human dignity, natural ingredients, and time-tested methods.

1. Ethical Artisan Wages

Our crafters set their own fair hourly and piece rates, ensuring generational craft sustainability without exploitative middlemen.

2. Earth-Friendly Raw Materials

Pure terracotta clay, unbleached cotton, wild harvested jute, virgin brass, and naturally aged fallen timber cost more than synthetic alternatives.

3. Time & Firing Cycles

A single double-fired ceramic piece requires up to 48 hours in a wood kiln. Handloom rugs require 10–14 days of continuous weaving.

4. Uniqueness & Zero Mass-Production

No high-speed hydraulic stamping presses or chemical vats. Every item reflects the singular hand signature of its creator.

5. Zero-Waste Studio Packaging

We invest in biodegradable honeycomb kraft paper, cotton ribbons, and durable multi-wall shipping boxes to safeguard delicate ceramics.

6. Community Reinvestment

A fixed percentage of proceeds funds clean water access, kiln maintenance, and apprentice stipends across artisan clusters.
