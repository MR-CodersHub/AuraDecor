The Story Behind This Vase

The Terracotta Sunrise Vase was born from Priya Sharma's daily walk past Jaipur's rooftops at dawn, watching the sky transform from deep indigo to warm amber. She sought to capture that ephemeral moment in clay â€” the way light seems to emanate from within warm terracotta.

Each vase begins as a ball of locally sourced red clay from Rajasthan's potter villages. Priya centers it on her wheel, drawing the walls upward with practiced hands before shaping the distinctive flared neck that defines this series.

The glaze â€” a proprietary blend of iron oxide and natural mineral pigments â€” is applied in three layers and fired twice in a wood-kiln to achieve the characteristic warm depth. No two vases carry exactly the same glaze pattern, making each one a unique artwork.

Meet the Maker

The Artisan
Behind This Piece

"Every time I sit at my wheel, I feel like the clay is telling me what it wants to become. My hands just help it get there."

Priya Sharma is a third-generation potter from Sanganer, one of Jaipur's oldest pottery villages. She learned the craft at her grandmother's wheel as a child, spending summer holidays coaxing clay into forms that would line their family home.

After formal training at the Rajasthan School of Arts, Priya returned to Sanganer with new techniques but the same reverence for tradition. She now leads a collective of 8 women potters, ensuring the craft passes to the next generation.

Her work has been exhibited in Delhi, Mumbai, and London, and each AuraDecor piece is personally signed on the base with her traditional potter's mark.
