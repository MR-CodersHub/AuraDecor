2 The Authentic Nature of Handmade Goods

Every product displayed on AuraDecor is handmade individually by Indian artisans using ancestral techniques including wheel-thrown pottery, natural pit-kiln firing, handloom pit weaving, lost-wax metal casting (Dhokra), and manual block printing.

Nuance is Not a Defect

Slight natural irregularities in clay glaze depth, wood grain patterns, thread tension, botanical dye undertones, and hand-chiseled accents are the genuine signatures of manual craftsmanship. They verify authentic origin and do not constitute manufacturing faults or grounds for defect claims.

Product photography represents typical craft runs under neutral studio lighting. While we strive for chromatic fidelity, color perception may vary slightly across individual digital screen displays.

3 Orders, Pricing & GST Compliance

All prices quoted on the website are displayed in Indian Rupees (₹ INR) and include all applicable Goods and Services Taxes (GST) under Indian statutory law.

  • Order Confirmation: An electronic order confirmation is issued via email immediately upon transaction success. This does not represent an accepted contract until the artisan studio confirms stock availability and inspection passes.
  • Fair Trade Pricing: Prices reflect fair, pre-negotiated living wages paid directly to our artisan masters. Discounts outside our published seasonal releases and wholesale tiers are not offered.
  • Pricing Corrections: In the rare instance of a typographical pricing error, AuraDecor reserves the right to contact the patron before dispatch or cancel the order with an immediate 100% refund.

4 Custom Commissions & Bespoke Orders

Patrons commissioning bespoke dimensions, non-standard ceramic glazes, corporate bulk gifting, or tailored architectural textiles are subject to specialized terms:

  1. Advance Deposits: Custom commissions require a 50% non-refundable advance deposit before raw material acquisition and kiln reservation commence.
  2. Artisan Lead Times: Custom pottery and handloom rugs typically require 2 to 5 weeks depending on atmospheric drying conditions and firing queues. Estimated delivery dates are collaborative targets rather than strict penalties.
  3. Non-Cancellable Status: Because bespoke items are handcrafted to individual architectural dimensions, custom commission orders cannot be refunded or cancelled once clay preparation or loom warp setup has begun.

5 Shipping & Fragile Transit Protocols

We take pride in exceptional, eco-conscious packaging designed specifically for fragile ceramics, blown glass, and carved woods:

  • Packaging Integrity: Over 98% of our packaging materials are plastic-free, utilizing double-walled corrugated craft boxes, honeycomb paper cushioning, and plant-starch packing chips.
  • Domestic Dispatch: Ready-to-ship catalog items are dispatched from our Jaipur studio within 3 to 5 business days. Transit typically spans 4 to 7 business days across India.
  • International Patrons: International orders ship via DHL Express or FedEx International Priority. Local customs duties, import tariffs, and clearance taxes levied by destination countries remain the responsibility of the patron.

6 Returns, Breakages & Refund Policy

We want you to cherish your handcrafted heirlooms. In the event of an issue, our return procedures are as follows:

  • Standard Catalog Returns: Standard catalog pieces may be returned within 7 days of delivery provided they remain unused, in original packaging, with unbroken artisan tags. Patrons cover return transit logistics unless the return arises from our error.
  • Transit Damage Guarantee: If a piece arrives damaged or broken during transit, notify us within 48 hours of delivery at hello@auradecor.in with photos of the damaged item and exterior packaging. We will provide an immediate replacement or full refund including original shipping charges.
  • Exclusions: Custom bespoke commissions, discounted sale archive items, and gift hampers with opened organic consumables are final sale and non-returnable.
