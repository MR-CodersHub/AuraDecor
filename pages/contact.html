Common Questions

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about our handmade products, shipping, returns, and artisan collaborations.

In-stock pieces ship within 3–5 business days from our Jaipur studio and typically arrive within 4–7 business days across India. Custom commissions or made-to-order pottery and handloom rugs require 2 to 4 weeks depending on the artisan's drying and firing schedules.
No. Slight variations in clay glaze tone, wood grain, weave density, and brushstrokes are the authentic signatures of human hands. We celebrate these unique nuances—they prove that your piece was individually created rather than stamped out in a factory.
Yes! We specialize in custom orders. Select "Custom Order" in the enquiry form above or visit our Pricing Page to see customization parameters and minimum lead times.
All ceramics and delicate crafts are packed using double-walled corrugated boxes, honeycomb kraft paper, shredded plant fibre cushioning, and reinforced corner protectors. Over 98% of our packaging is 100% recyclable and completely plastic-free.
We accept returns within 7 days of delivery for standard items in unused, original condition with artisan tags intact. Custom bespoke orders and personalized items are non-refundable unless damaged during transit.
Yes, we ship to over 35 countries via trusted courier partners including DHL Express and FedEx. International shipping costs and duty estimates are provided during checkout or upon custom quote request.
AuraDecor adheres strictly to fair trade guidelines. Artisans receive upfront advance payments for materials and fair, dignified prices set in direct collaboration with guild elders and master makers. Over 65% of net product proceeds flow directly back to artisan communities.
We welcome guests to our central studio and arrange curated heritage studio walks in Sanganer and surrounding craft clusters by prior appointment. Please submit a request via our form at least 5 days in advance.
