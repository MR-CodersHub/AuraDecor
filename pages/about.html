Our Story

Born from a Love of Handmade

It began with a journey. Our founders — avid travellers and design lovers — spent months visiting artisan villages across Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Kerala. In workshop after workshop, they found something extraordinary: skilled people creating objects of genuine beauty, using techniques their grandparents had taught them.

What struck them most was not just the craftsmanship — it was the stories. Every potter, weaver, and woodcarver had a lineage, a tradition, a living connection to their craft. And yet many of these artisans struggled to reach the customers who would treasure their work.

AuraDecor was born from that realisation. Established in Jaipur, we set out to build a bridge between India's extraordinary artisan communities and the homes that would be enriched by their work. Today, we are proud to work with over 200 artisans across 12 states, bringing authentic handmade treasures to contemporary homes.

"Every handmade piece carries the energy and intention of its maker."

— Meera Nair, Co-Founder, AuraDecor
Why We Exist

Our Mission & Philosophy

Four principles guide everything we do at AuraDecor.

Supporting Artisans

We believe in fair compensation, long-term partnerships, and genuine artisan empowerment. When artisans thrive, their craft survives — and that is good for everyone.

Preserving Traditions

Many of India's greatest craft traditions are at risk. We work to keep ancient techniques alive — not as museum pieces, but as living, relevant skills for contemporary homes.

Celebrating Individuality

Each handmade piece is unique, like the person who makes it and the person who chooses it. We celebrate the imperfections, variations, and character that only handcraft can offer.

Handcrafted for Modern Homes

Traditional craft does not belong only in museums. We curate pieces that bring artisan beauty into contemporary living spaces — bridging centuries of skill with today's design sensibilities.

The Craft

Craftsmanship & Materials

Natural Materials, Naturally Beautiful

We work with the finest natural materials — mineral-rich terracotta clay, hand-spun cotton and jute, reclaimed hardwoods, and traditional metal alloys like brass and copper. These are materials that have been used by Indian artisans for centuries, and their qualities — warmth, weight, texture, and character — cannot be replicated by machines.

  • Natural terracotta and stoneware clay
  • Hand-spun cotton, jute, and natural fibres
  • Mango, sheesham, and reclaimed hardwoods
  • Brass, copper, and iron — traditionally worked

Traditional Techniques, Modern Homes

Block printing, wheel-throwing, hand-weaving, wood carving, lost-wax casting, natural dyeing — these techniques are centuries old, yet they produce objects of extraordinary contemporary beauty. Our artisans have mastered these methods through years of practice, and their expertise is evident in every finished piece.

  • Wheel-throwing and hand-building in pottery
  • Handloom weaving and block printing
  • Traditional wood carving and joinery
  • Lost-wax casting and hand-hammered metalwork
