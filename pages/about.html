Living with Handmade

Honouring & Caring for Your Pieces

Authentic natural materials age with dignity. With gentle, mindful care, your AuraDecor pieces will develop a richer patina and last for generations.

Terracotta & Stoneware

Hand-wash with lukewarm water and mild organic soap. Avoid harsh chemical abrasive pads that strip the hand-burnished slip.

Allow terracotta to dry completely in open air before nesting or storing.

Handloom Textiles & Jute

Spot-clean or dry-clean handloom pieces. For natural jute rugs, vacuum gently without a beater brush along the direction of weave.

Keep natural vegetable-dyed fabrics away from prolonged direct harsh sunlight.

Solid Reclaimed Hardwoods

Dust with a soft dry microfiber cloth. Rejuvenate the wood luster once or twice annually with pure cold-pressed beeswax polish.

Never place wood decor in direct contact with wet standing water or heaters.

Lost-Wax Brass & Copper

Clean naturally with a gentle paste of tamarind or lemon juice and salt, rinse thoroughly, and buff with a soft cotton lint-free towel.

Embrace the mellow golden patina that develops naturally as metal breathes air.
