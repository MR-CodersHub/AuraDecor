The Potter's Wheel:
Priya Sharma's Journey

Priya Sharma · Jaipur, Rajasthan · 8 min read

In a sunlit workshop tucked into the lanes of Sanganer, Priya Sharma begins her day before dawn. The wheel spins, the clay rises, and three generations of pottery tradition flow through her hands. We visit her workspace to discover how ancient technique meets contemporary design — and why she believes every vessel holds a conversation.

"My grandmother said the clay teaches patience. You can't rush a pot, any more than you can rush a child. It tells you when it's ready," Priya shares, her fingers still bearing the warm-red stain of the morning's first clay.
