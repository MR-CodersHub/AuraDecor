Privacy Policy for
AuraDecor Patrons & Makers

1 Overview & Our Craft Ethos

AuraDecor ("we", "our", or "us"), headquartered in Jaipur, Rajasthan, operates an ethical artisan marketplace and heritage craft curation studio connecting discerning homeowners, designers, and collectors with India's master makers.

This Privacy Policy explains how we gather, store, handle, and protect your information when you visit our website (auradecor.in), purchase handcrafted goods, commission bespoke designs, submit workshop inquiries, or subscribe to our Workshop Notes publication.

Zero Data Selling Pledge

AuraDecor will never sell, rent, lease, or monetize your personal details or buying habits to third-party ad brokers or data aggregators. Every piece of information we retain exists purely to fulfill your orders and support authentic artisan livelihoods.

2 Information We Collect

Depending on how you interact with our studio and digital storefront, we may collect the following categories of data:

  • Patron Identity & Contact: Full legal name, shipping address, billing address, email address, and telephone/WhatsApp contact number.
  • Transactional Records: Order identifiers, cart line items, bespoke dimension requests, custom glaze specifications, gift messages, and delivery tracking updates.
  • Payment Information: All payment card numbers, UPI handles, and net banking credentials are processed directly through PCI-DSS Level 1 certified payment gateways. AuraDecor does not store your raw CVV or card numbers on our servers.
  • Custom Commission Inquiries: Architectural floorplans, room dimensions, wood species preferences, and color palette references uploaded via our Custom Order & Pricing portal.
  • Technical & Browsing Logs: Anonymized IP addresses, browser engine versions, device screen resolutions, preferred color scheme (dark mode / light mode), and text orientation (LTR / RTL).

3 How We Use Your Information

We process collected patron data strictly for legitimate commerce and craft fulfillment purposes:

  1. Order Execution & Dispatch: Packing, scheduling fragile courier transport via verified logistical partners (DHL, FedEx, Blue Dart), and delivering order transit notifications.
  2. Artisan Studio Liaison: Coordinating bespoke dimensions and firing timelines directly with our guild masters across Rajasthan, Gujarat, and Bihar.
  3. Patron Care & Warranty: Assisting with ceramic care instructions, natural dye cleaning procedures, damaged transit claims, and returns.
  4. Studio Communications: Sending monthly Workshop Notes, seasonal kiln release previews, and studio tour confirmations (patrons may unsubscribe with a single click at any time).
  5. Platform Security & Compliance: Safeguarding against fraudulent payment attempts, preventing malicious bot behavior, and maintaining GST accounting records.

4 Artisan & Third-Party Sharing

Because every AuraDecor piece is made by human hands, order fulfillment involves select trusted partners:

  • Artisan Cooperatives: Master potters and handloom weavers receive order dimensions, design schematics, and first names for customized authenticity signature seals. Full home addresses and phone numbers are never disclosed to artisan workshops.
  • Logistics Partners: Verified shipping carriers receive patron addresses and delivery contact numbers solely for route navigation and OTP delivery verification.
  • Payment Processors: Encrypted transaction handshakes with regulated Indian banking gateways (Razorpay / Stripe) to securely finalize purchases.
  • Legal & Statutory Authorities: Information is only disclosed if mandated by Indian law, court order, or official tax auditing requirements.

5 Cookies & Local Storage

We use essential cookies and browser LocalStorage strictly to provide a smooth, enjoyable browsing session:

  • Cart Persistence: Retains your selected handcrafted pottery and textiles across browser tabs so your cart does not vanish.
  • Theme Preference: Remembers your chosen Dark Mode or Light Mode display theme.
  • Language & RTL: Stores your reading direction (Left-to-Right or Right-to-Left) for seamless international navigation.
  • Anonymous Analytics: Aggregated, privacy-respecting page counts to understand which artisan stories resonate most with readers.

6 Security & Data Encryption

All network traffic across auradecor.in is encrypted end-to-end using Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / 256-bit SSL). Our cloud databases reside in enterprise-grade ISO 27001 certified data centers with continuous automated penetration testing, strict access role segregation, and redundant encrypted backups.

Transparent Breach Protocol

In the highly improbable event of any security vulnerability or breach impacting patron records, AuraDecor will notify all affected patrons and relevant regulatory authorities within 72 hours of verification.

7 Your Privacy Rights

Under applicable Indian and international digital privacy frameworks (including the Digital Personal Data Protection Act), you enjoy full control over your personal data:

  • Right to Access: Request a complete copy of all personal records and past purchase logs linked to your account.
  • Right to Rectification: Update or correct outdated delivery addresses, recipient names, or phone numbers.
  • Right to Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten"): Request full deletion of your customer profile, newsletter subscription, and saved preferences.
  • Opt-Out of Marketing: Every newsletter email includes a one-click unsubscribe mechanism that takes effect immediately.
