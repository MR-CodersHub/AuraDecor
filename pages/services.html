The Bespoke Process

From Initial Sketch to Hearthside Heirlooms

A patient, collaborative journey honouring centuries-old artisanal slowcraft.

01

Vision & Consultation

Share your room photos, dimensions, and preferred craft tradition. Our design team aligns on the material palette and crafts a preliminary estimate.

Step 1 · Complimentary
02

Artisan Swatches

Our master artisan prepares clay test tiles, textile dye swatches, or scale charcoal sketches for your approval before material shaping begins.

Step 2 · Design Sign-Off
03

Patient Creation

Hand-thrown, loomed, or lost-wax cast over 2–4 weeks. We send high-resolution photo and video dispatches straight from the master's studio floor.

Step 3 · Studio Updates
04

Crated Delivery

Delivered in shockproof eco-timber packaging with a signed Certificate of Authenticity and the master artisan's handwritten dedication.

Step 4 · Safe Insured Arrival

Handloom Textiles

Bespoke Kilims & Hallway Runners

Hand-knotted from indigenous organic wool and jute, dyed with indigo, turmeric, and fermented iron-mud.

  • Max Dimensions: Up to 24ft Custom Length
  • Lead Time: 3–5 Weeks
  • Guild: Kutch Weavers Guild
